About Me

Welcome to my personal website. I am an AI researcher and developer with a deep focus on medical imaging, physics-informed neural networks, and intelligent diagnostic systems.

My work bridges the gap between cutting-edge machine learning research and real-world clinical applications — from brain tumour segmentation to COVID-19 diagnosis using MRI fingerprinting.

I am passionate about building tools that make a meaningful difference in healthcare, and I enjoy collaborating across disciplines to push the boundaries of what AI can achieve.
